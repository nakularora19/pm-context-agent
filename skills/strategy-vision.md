# Strategy & Vision

## When to use this skill

Use once per area per year, or when strategy materially shifts, with OKRs refreshed each planning period. This is the top of the document stack: roadmap and opportunity scoping documents link up to it.

Target length: one page. If it runs longer, cut it; a strategy no one rereads does not steer decisions.

## Inputs required

- The company or business-unit strategy this must support.
- Current metrics and trends for the area, recent research, and competitive signals.
- The OKR framework for the period.

Follow `AGENTS.md` for context loading, source ownership, and shared-document approval. Use `pm-profile.md` for output preferences, `context/current-quarter.md` for recorded metrics and OKRs, and `context/team-charter.md` when mission or scope matters.

Read linked strategy and evidence sources when available. If access is unavailable, request the relevant content. Flag missing inputs rather than inventing strategic direction, evidence, baselines, or targets. Cite grounding and distinguish inference from established facts.

Infer this workflow from a request to define or refresh an area's direction; no special invocation is required. Use Draft: sectioned, writing one or two sections and pausing for review, unless the PM explicitly requests a full draft.

## What the document contains

Keep the whole document to one page, with five parts:

1. **Vision:** One sentence describing the multi-year end state for users if the strategy succeeds. Not a paragraph.
2. **Focus (the bets):** Two or three bets, each with its supporting insight in one line. Make choices rather than treating everything as a priority.
3. **Metrics that matter:** The north star metric plus two or three inputs that move it. Every roadmap bet should trace to one of these.
4. **OKRs (this period):** One to three objectives, each with a couple of measurable key results expressed as baseline → target. Mark unknown values for confirmation.
5. **Not doing:** A short list of what the team deliberately excludes during this horizon.

## Output format

Produce a one-page Markdown document with the five parts above as headings. Link to supporting research and competitive detail instead of pasting it in. Do not invent links or imply that an inaccessible source has been reviewed.

Keep vision and focus stable across the year unless strategy materially shifts. During routine period refreshes, update the OKRs; flag changes to assumptions or metrics that may require a wider strategy review.

Draft in the conversation first. Update a shared strategy document only after the PM approves the draft. Flag conflicts with recorded quarterly goals rather than silently rewriting the plan.

## Notes for adapting this skill

- One sentence for the vision, one page for the whole document.
- Use no more than three objectives; the period is not a wish list.
- Put supporting detail in linked documents so the strategy stays concise.
- Link the roadmap to this strategy and its current-period OKRs; keep project scope and requirements in their own documents.
